# The demo instrument

This repository ships a working instrument so that "build a flow" has something
to mean before you have written one. It is ours, not a template you are meant to
keep: the point is that you can deploy it, receive it on your own phone, and read
the code that produced it.

## What it is

`flows/data_use_demo_en.json`, generated by `scripts/build_data_use_demo.py`. It
is a methods demonstration: respondents are randomised into two arms that get the
**same six questions** in different formats, and the closing message reveals the
experiment.

- **ARM 1** asks in dense prose and demands a typed answer.
- **ARM 2** asks the same things in plain language as a tappable list, warm in
  tone, with a progress cue on every question.

**ARM 2 is the pattern this repo recommends** — it exists to be copied. ARM 1
exists so the difference between them can be measured rather than asserted, which
is the whole reason the demo randomises rather than simply showing the better
version.

Most rounds are not experiments and should not carry an `arm` at all. Nothing in
`src/` depends on it.

## Both languages, one structure

English and Spanish come from one structure and two string tables, so a fix to
the graph lands in both and neither can quietly drift from the other. Both flows
are committed - `flows/data_use_demo_en.json` and `_es.json` - and both build
from the same code.

The builder resolves your Twilio Functions service and content templates **by
name**, so `just build-demo-flow` produces a flow that points at your account
rather than at ours. Either language needs its two bookend templates to exist on
your account first; the build stops and names them if they do not.

```powershell
just demo-templates-create        # the in-session templates the questions need
just build-demo-flow --lang en    # writes flows/data_use_demo_en.json
just flow-deploy flows/data_use_demo_en.json --publish
```

Only the bookends — the opener and the closing message to someone who never
replied — need Meta approval. Everything in between is in-session and free.

## The counter-example

`flows/foro_nacional_datos_source.json` is an as-ran original, kept unmodified as
provenance. It is **not runnable here** — its templates and Functions live on a
different account — but `just flow-check flows/foro_nacional_datos_source.json`
reads it off disk and reports real defects in it. It is kept precisely because it
still fails, which makes it the honest thing to run the checks against before you
point them at your own flow.
